# Hawaii Trip — Local Planner

Simple static site to record flight times and car reservations for a Hawaii trip. Data is stored locally in your browser's `localStorage`.

Usage
- Open `index.html` in your browser.
- Fill the Flight or Car Reservation forms and click the add button.
- Entries are shown below each form. Use Delete to remove an entry.

- Hotel reservations: fill the Hotel Reservation form and add entries; they appear under "Saved Hotel Reservations".

- Thumbnails for each record are now graphic icons that scale properly and look crisp, even on mobile. Drag dashboard cards to reorder them.

Notes
- This is a local-only demo (no server). To share or persist across devices, export/import or connect to a backend.
